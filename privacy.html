1. Introduction

CoolCare AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, register as a repair shop, or interact with our AI-powered WhatsApp assistant.

By using CoolCare AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services immediately.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data depending on how you interact with our platform:

Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Shop name, owner name, business address, city, email address, mobile phone number, and password (encrypted).
  • Business Details: Service areas, types of appliance repairs offered, technician roster, business hours, and pricing information.
  • Communication & Support: Any information you share when contacting our support team via email, WhatsApp, or contact forms.
  • Payment Information: Billing details and transaction history. Note: All payment processing is handled by third-party gateways (e.g., Razorpay) and we do not store full credit/debit card numbers.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, referral source, and interactions with our AI assistant.
  • Device & Browser Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and approximate geographic location (city/region level).
  • Cookies & Tracking: See the Cookies & Analytics section below for details.

Customer Data (Processed on Behalf of Repair Shops)

  • When end customers interact with a repair shop via our WhatsApp assistant, we process their phone number, name (if shared), location/area, appliance issue description, communication history, and preferred appointment times.
  • This data is processed on behalf of the repair shop (as a data processor). The repair shop is the data controller for this information.

3. How We Collect Your Data

We collect data through the following channels:

  • Direct Registration: When you sign up as a repair shop through our signup form.
  • WhatsApp API Integration: Through Meta's WhatsApp Business API when customers message your connected WhatsApp number (see Section 4).
  • Website Interaction: Through cookies, analytics tools, and form submissions on coolcare.zeeshstudios.in.
  • Communication: Email and WhatsApp correspondence with our support and sales teams.

4. WhatsApp Business Data

CoolCare AI connects to Meta's (Facebook's) WhatsApp Business API to enable the AI assistant functionality. Here's what you need to know about WhatsApp-related data:

  • Meta Embedded Signup: Repair shop owners connect their own WhatsApp Business Account using Meta's Embedded Signup flow. This is a OAuth-based process managed directly by Meta.
  • Data Shared with Meta: When you connect your WhatsApp Business Account, certain information (business name, phone number, profile photo, and business verification status) is shared between Meta and CoolCare AI as necessary to provide the service.
  • Message Content: CoolCare AI stores WhatsApp message content (both inbound from customers and outbound AI replies) to maintain conversation context, enable booking creation, and improve the AI assistant. This data is encrypted at rest.
  • Customer Phone Numbers: Phone numbers of customers who message your shop are stored and associated with conversation histories and booking records.
  • Webhook Events: Meta sends webhook events (message receipts, delivery status, and message content) to our servers to process conversations in real-time.

For more information on how Meta handles your data, please refer to the Meta Privacy Policy and Meta's Business Tools Terms.

5. How We Store Your Data

  • Database: Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon Serverless Postgres, which is fully SOC 2 compliant and encrypted at rest (AES-256).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device, our servers, and third-party services (including Meta's APIs) is encrypted using TLS 1.2+.
  • Sensitive Credentials: WhatsApp access tokens and other sensitive credentials are encrypted using AES-256-CBC before being stored in our database. Decryption keys are never stored alongside encrypted data.
  • Data Segregation: Each repair shop's data is logically segregated. Customer conversation data is associated with the specific shop's WhatsApp connection.
  • Server Locations: Our infrastructure is hosted on Vercel's Edge Network and Neon's cloud infrastructure, primarily in the US and EU regions.

6. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Powering the AI WhatsApp assistant, managing bookings, sending notifications, and providing the dashboard.
  • Account Management: Managing your shop registration, subscription billing, and account settings.
  • Communication: Sending service-related messages (booking confirmations, reminders, invoices) and responding to support inquiries.
  • Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve our AI assistant's accuracy, user experience, and feature development.
  • Legal Compliance: Fulfilling legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing our terms of service.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or customer data to third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate (Neon for database, Vercel for hosting, Razorpay/Stripe for payments, Meta for WhatsApp API, Groq for AI inference). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request (e.g., court order, subpoena).
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — you will be notified via email and a prominent notice on our website.
  • With Your Consent: When you have given explicit consent for a specific sharing purpose.

8. Cookies & Analytics

CoolCare AI uses minimal cookies and analytics to improve your experience. Here's what we use:

Essential Cookies

  • Authentication Tokens: Stored in browser localStorage (not cookies) to maintain your logged-in session. These are cleared when you log out.
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your selected currency, billing cycle, and country preference across visits.

Analytics

  • Vercel Analytics: We may use Vercel's built-in analytics to understand website traffic patterns. This is privacy-friendly and does not track individual users across sites.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any cross-site tracking cookies.

Your Cookie Choices

Since we only use essential cookies and privacy-friendly analytics, no cookie consent banner is required. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the platform may not work properly.

9. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained for as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after account deletion to allow for data recovery and legal compliance.
  • Conversation History: WhatsApp conversation logs are retained for the duration of your subscription plus 30 days.
  • Booking Records: Retained for 3 years for business record-keeping purposes.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for product improvement. Personally identifiable analytics data is retained for 26 months.
  • Legal Holds: Data may be retained longer if required by applicable law or if there is an outstanding legal request.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mazeeshan25104@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

11. How to Request Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your data in two ways:

  • From Your Dashboard: Log in to your shop dashboard and navigate to Settings → Delete Account. You can initiate the deletion process directly.
  • By Email: Send a deletion request to mazeeshan25104@gmail.com from your registered email address. We will process your request within 15 business days.
  • From Our Data Deletion Page: Visit our dedicated Data Deletion Request page for more detailed instructions.

Note: When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data, WhatsApp connection credentials, conversation logs, and booking history within 90 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (e.g., financial transaction records).

12. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security measures, including:

  • End-to-end encryption (TLS 1.2+) for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest (passwords, tokens)
  • Encrypted storage of WhatsApp access tokens using AES-256-CBC
  • Regular security audits and dependency vulnerability scans
  • Rate limiting and DDoS protection on all API endpoints
  • Strict access controls — only authorized personnel have access to production data
  • Subscriptions with automatic security updates for all dependencies
