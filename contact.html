Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password." Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.
How do I connect my WhatsApp number?
After registration, go to AI Settings in your dashboard. You'll find the WhatsApp configuration section where you can connect your WhatsApp Business API credentials.
Why was my account rejected?
Accounts are reviewed to ensure quality service. If yours was rejected, it may be due to incomplete information. Contact us at support@coolcare.ai and we'll help resolve it.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from the Subscription page in your dashboard. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period.
