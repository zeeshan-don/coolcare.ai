FAQ

Common questions.

How does the WhatsApp bot work?
When a customer messages your business WhatsApp number, CoolCare's AI assistant automatically replies, gathers the problem details, location, and preferred time slot, then creates a booking in your dashboard.
Do I need a separate WhatsApp number?
No. CoolCare connects to your existing WhatsApp Business account through the Meta API. Your customers keep messaging the same number they already know.
Can I customize the AI responses?
Yes. You can configure the greeting message, business hours, supported services, knowledge base, fallback responses, and when to transfer to a human agent — all from the AI Settings page.
What happens if my subscription expires?
Your dashboard remains accessible in view-only mode. The WhatsApp bot stops creating new bookings. You can renew anytime and pick up right where you left off.
Can I change my billing cycle?
Absolutely. You can switch between monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and yearly billing at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a referral program?
Yes! Every shop gets a unique referral code. When another shop signs up using your code and subscribes, you earn a $10 credit. Check the Referrals page in your dashboard.
