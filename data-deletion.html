What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently erased or anonymized:

Data Category Details Deleted?
Account Credentials Email, password hash, mobile number ✓ Yes
Shop Profile Shop name, address, city, service areas, owner name ✓ Yes
WhatsApp Connection Access tokens, WABA ID, phone number ID, webhook status ✓ Yes
WhatsApp Messages Conversation history, customer messages, AI replies ✓ Yes
Conversation State Active conversation context (customer_number, step, flow) ✓ Yes
Booking Records Booking history, appointments, technician assignments ✓ Yes
Customer Data Customer phone numbers, names, addresses shared via WhatsApp ✓ Yes
Technician Roster Technician names, phone numbers, service types ✓ Yes
Subscription History Plan type, billing cycle, payment history ● Anonymized
Referral Data Referral codes, referral credits ✓ Yes
Analytics (Personal) IP-based usage logs associated with your account ● Anonymized
Financial Records Transaction invoices, payment gateway receipts Retained (legal)

Note: Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by applicable tax and financial regulations. These records are anonymized (your personal information is removed, but the transaction record itself is kept).

Deletion Timeline

Here's what happens after you submit a deletion request:

Day 1–2

We receive your request, verify your identity, and send a confirmation email.

Day 3–7

Your account is deactivated. Dashboard access is revoked. WhatsApp bot stops responding.

Day 8–15

All personal data is permanently deleted from our primary database and backups.

Day 90

Anonymized financial records are the only remaining data. All caches and CDN copies purged.

⚠️

Irreversible Action: Account deletion is permanent. All bookings, conversations, dashboard data, and WhatsApp connection settings will be lost. This action cannot be undone. If you're unsure, you can cancel your subscription instead — your data will remain available in view-only mode.

Data Deletion for End Customers

If you are a customer who has interacted with a repair shop through CoolCare AI's WhatsApp assistant, and you would like your data deleted, please follow these steps:

  • Contact the Shop Directly: Your data is primarily controlled by the repair shop you messaged. Contact them and request deletion.
  • Contact CoolCare AI: If you're unable to reach the shop, email us at mazeeshan25104@gmail.com with your phone number and the shop's name. We will process your request within 15 business days.
  • WhatsApp Opt-Out: Reply "STOP" to any message from the AI assistant to opt out of future messages. Your existing conversation data will still be retained until the shop deletes it or requests deletion.

What gets deleted: Your phone number, conversation history, and any personal details (name, address, appliance details) shared during the WhatsApp conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the deletion process take?
We process all deletion requests within 15 business days. Most requests are completed within 7 days. Financial records are retained in anonymized form for 7 years to comply with tax regulations.
Will my WhatsApp Business Account be affected?
No. Your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) is owned by you and managed through Meta. Deleting your CoolCare AI account only removes the connection tokens from our system. Your WABA remains active. If you want to fully disconnect, you can do so from Meta's Business Settings portal.
Can I recover my data after requesting deletion?
Once the deletion process is complete (after 15 days), data cannot be recovered. If you change your mind within 48 hours of your request, contact us immediately and we may be able to cancel the deletion if processing has not yet completed.
What if I just want to cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription from the Subscription page in your dashboard. Your data remains available in view-only mode. You can resubscribe anytime to reactivate the full service. Deleting your account is separate from cancelling your subscription.
Does deletion also remove data from Meta's servers?
No. Deleting your data from CoolCare AI does not delete data from Meta (Facebook/WhatsApp) servers. Meta has its own data retention policies. You can manage your WhatsApp data through Meta's Privacy Settings. Please refer to Meta's Privacy Policy for details.
